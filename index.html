Legal review before termination

The most useful legal advice often comes before the first termination notice is sent.

We focus on reconstructing the commercial relationship from its documents and actual performance, identifying available legal routes and assessing their consequences before a decision is implemented.

The question is not only whether the relationship can end, but how to manage its legal and commercial consequences.

Documents and facts to examine

  • Original distribution agreement, amendments and renewal communications
  • Emails, purchase orders and the parties’ conduct after contractual expiry
  • Sales targets, performance reports and prior breach notices
  • Exclusivity commitments and market-development arrangements
  • Outstanding invoices, inventory and open purchase orders
  • Governing-law, jurisdiction and dispute-resolution provisions

The scope of any review depends on the documents and circumstances of the specific relationship.

Argentine law and cross-border contracts

Distribution arrangements involving Argentina may raise questions of contract characterization, applicable law, forum selection, termination without cause, material breach and the evidentiary significance of subsequent conduct.

Where Argentine law is applicable, the Civil and Commercial Code contains provisions relevant to concession and distribution relationships, including rules concerning duration and termination. Their application must be examined in light of the contract and the circumstances. In international matters, governing-law and jurisdiction clauses require separate analysis.

This page provides general information, not a conclusion about any particular contract or an assurance that Argentine law will govern a given dispute.
